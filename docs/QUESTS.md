# Quest authoring and tracker

Create an example from **Menu > New quest adventure**. It creates a new scene through the usual save/discard protection. Press Play. The right-side tracker already lists the quest and its objectives; click its title for story/details. Click Close to return. The compact tracker shows quests and a cogwheel only. Clicking any quest title or objective opens a wider journal with quest list, description, current step and creator-written hints. Journal includes untracked quests; details offer Pin/Unpin. The cogwheel contains background opacity, Unlock to move / Lock position, Reset position, Collapse/Expand and journal access. Drag the empty top area after unlocking, then lock it. Position is clamped within the available window and restored after relaunch. Collapse hides objective rows without changing gameplay. Wheel or arrow keys scroll; Enter opens the first quest, J switches journal view. There is no animated UI effect, so reduced motion needs no alternative animation.

In **Adventure controls > Quests**, select an existing quest and Load, or create a new one. Enter its title and multiline description, select objective rules by name (Ctrl-click for several), and Save. Rule names supply objective text. Add the underlying collection, interaction or area rules in the Gameplay Rules section. Required-item conditions supply collection progress; prior-objective conditions express staged quests. A final interaction rule can represent reporting back to an NPC or terminal. Save, removal and edits support Undo.

Definitions use stable IDs, up to32 quests and16 linked objective rules per quest, within the existing64-rule limit. Scene schema10 saves quest metadata. Earlier scenes remain supported; rules without explicit quest definitions appear individually in the tracker. Definition save/reload and export preserve links. The renderer's editor and standalone player share quest_model.hpp and quest_tracker.hpp. Actual rule execution drives progress; the tracker never awards completion. Duplicate collection keys count once. Reset starts an independent Play session; authored data remains unchanged.

Current scope: quests are active from scene start, with prerequisite-locked objectives. No accept/abandon NPC dialogue, multiplayer/server authority, player-progress save slots, reward economy or map/navigation links are implemented. Pin/collapse are presentation choices for the current Play session. Background opacity (0–100% in10% increments), locked state and normalized tracker position persist per user in quest-tracker.json; only the backdrop fades, not text. The journal opens separately within the wider panel and does not overwrite the compact tracker position. Author definitions save with scenes; that is distinct from saving a player's completed quest progress.

Startup: editor and visible standalone player request foreground activation once. They do not remain topmost or reclaim focus after the user switches applications. Hidden export checks stay hidden. Visible checks abort if foreground activation fails or focus is lost, requiring user direction before retry.

Evidence (24 September2026):181 native checks;20 final authoring checks;12 final local export checks;25 visible tracker checks;two independent editor processes verify opacity, lock and custom position persistence. ## Grouped objectives (24 September 2026)

In Adventure controls, load/create a quest and select its objective rules. Objectives per step accepts counts such as `5,1`: the first five selected rules form step one, and the sixth forms step two. Each count must be1–5 and the counts must cover the selection; the existing16-objective total limit remains. Leave blank to preserve legacy quest behavior. New selections follow their displayed rule order; loading and resaving preserves saved objective order. Save/Undo/Redo and export preserve groups (schema11).

Objectives within a step can be completed in any order. The runtime prevents later steps from firing until every earlier objective has completed. Same-step and future-step objective prerequisites are rejected. Compact tracking shows the current step; the journal retains all steps. Completed objectives turn green with a check and strikethrough. Restart clears progress without changing author definitions.

The later F-menu extension below adds object authoring and creation lessons. EXP and rewards remain Task6.

## F object menu and creation lessons

In walk-and-build, face a block within three metres and press F. In the orbit editor, point the cursor at an object and press F. The menu offers Carry / move, Open (remove barrier), Breakable, Collect into inventory, No interaction, quest-objective creation, stand-on objective creation, and full object properties. F and E both activate gameplay objects in Play and the exported player.

Carry is an editor placement operation: choose Carry, move to the desired location, then press F to place. Escape cancels. Preview movement does not change the authored scene until placement. Placement is one Undo operation and preserves identity and quest links. Open and Break currently remove the object (and its collision); they are single-use actions, not hinge animation or fractured debris. Collect removes the item into inventory; it is distinct from editor Carry.

Choose Build > Learn: three blocks for a tutorial with three different new cubes. Each line first asks for placement, then F, then that cube's own requirement: Open, Breakable, or Carry to another spot. Complete the three in any order. A cube is reserved to its objective and cannot satisfy another by changing properties. Prior objects do not count; changing the wrong cube does not count. Undoing or deleting required work removes its completion. Two optional single-property lessons also remain available. Creation lessons are session-local, not saved player progress or EXP; rewards and a longer unlock chain remain Task6. The lesson controller accepts1–5 distinct-object requirements for future lessons; the available combined tutorial uses three.

Add quest objective creates/extends the saved Object interactions quest with the selected object's real interaction. Preset changes keep its stable rule link and update its expected event; this never awards completion by itself. Clearing an attached behavior is rejected until its objective is removed. Gameplay progress is still independent from editing and resets on Play restart.
