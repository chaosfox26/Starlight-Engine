# Starlight camera and movement requirements

Hold right mouse button to turn camera and character together; left drag orbits independently. Release restores cursor interaction. Wheel zoom transitions third person into first person. WASD moves, A/D strafe, Space jumps and a second fresh airborne press double-jumps. Held Space does not repeat jumps. Landing resets jumps, including on moving platforms. Preserve collision and safe camera behavior.

The Step 4 specification governs current controls, values, and acceptance. This note is not a completion report.
