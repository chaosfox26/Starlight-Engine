Supported source objects include primitives, sculpts/imported GLB, grass, fire, lights, lava, lightning, rocks, light shards and smoke; terrain supports water and liquid sources. Standalone cloth, deformable soft bodies and ragdolls are not implemented and have no detailed authored behavior in the current specification. They must not be represented as completed features. Motion vectors belong to rendering rather than a new physical force.

Priority: finish remaining task1 implementation and verification; task2 meshes/materials follows; task3 performance remains last. This document is an evidence inventory, not completion approval.
